# Initial risk register

Status: **All risks OPEN** at Milestone 3. Severity is a preliminary design judgment, not measured incident frequency or residual-risk acceptance. Owners are fictional operating-model roles from the [charter](project-charter.md); controls marked planned are not deployed. Reassess on policy/model/tool/service changes and before any readiness claim.

| ID / severity | Risk and affected requirements | Current evidence / planned response | Owner and closure evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-001 / High | Ambiguous or wrong policy/endorsement version leads to an unsupported recommendation. FR-004, FR-005, FR-006, FR-011 | Metadata/citation matching exists; no approved policy register or resolver. Planned: unique approved bundle, version-pinned retrieval/rules, abstention and supervisor exception assignment. | Policy owner with supervisor; approved fixtures and VT-004/VT-005/VT-006/VT-011 evidence. |
| R-002 / High | Ungrounded model conclusions or excessive human trust cause a misleading/adverse outcome. FR-007, FR-008, FR-009, FR-010, FR-011 | Local human/supervisor guards exist; agents/support validation/UI do not. Planned: inspectable citations, source verification, explicit uncertainty, review/override reasons and simpler-baseline comparison. | Claims supervisor; VT-007–VT-011 with unsupported assertions rejected and preserved review evidence. |
| R-003 / High | OCR/photo/document errors or missing artifacts become canonical facts. FR-002, FR-003, FR-012 | Local reference/page/hash/scan-assertion checks exist; no scanner, byte verification or extractor. Planned: quarantine, verified hashes, approved completeness/quality checks, source inspection and reassessment after corrections. | Claims handler; VT-002/VT-003/VT-012, including poor-quality and conflicting evidence. |
| R-004 / High | Prompt injection in uploads/retrieval/tool output changes instructions or gains tool/decision authority. FR-015, NFR-003 | Supplied flags trigger local escalation; detection/tool authorization is absent. Planned: content isolation, bounded allowlisted tools, backend grants, egress restrictions and attack tests. | Security/privacy reviewer; VT-015/VT-018 and documented residual limitations. |
| R-005 / High | Real/private data enters fixtures, logs, indexes or audit access; deletion/access assumptions fail. NFR-001, NFR-002 | Synthetic-only markers and package log redaction exist; no provenance certification, end-to-end auth or deletion enforcement. Planned: generated data provenance, accidental-upload isolation, least privilege, redaction/access/deletion tests and incident procedure. | Compliance/audit with security/privacy; VT-016/VT-017, approved retention and authorized audit-read evidence. |
| R-006 / High | Unavailable model quota/region/APIM/dependencies, concurrency or restart loses work or invites unsafe fallback. FR-013, NFR-004, NFR-005, NFR-008 | Local recovery edges and protocols exist; no live capacity/persistence tests. Planned: compatibility gate, bounded retry/deadline, atomic outbox/audit, replay protection and owned alerts; never widen policy scope or bypass APIM. | Platform operator; VT-013/VT-019/VT-020/VT-023 and later restore evidence. |
| R-007 / Medium | Token loops, fixed service charges, telemetry or idle resources exceed the portfolio budget. NFR-008 | Local settings are not a budget control; no cost ceiling is approved or spend measured. Planned: approved monthly/run budgets, model/tool/page limits, usage suspension, expiration/cleanup and residual-cost checks. | Platform operator with sponsor; VT-023 and approved cost assumptions; budget notifications alone are insufficient. |

Risk closure requires the named owner to review actual evidence and residual limitations; a new document, protocol or passing schema test alone does not close a risk. Target cost/capacity controls remain in the [deployment strategy](../deployment/deployment-strategy.md), safety/privacy controls in [security and governance](../architecture/security-and-governance.md), and future test specifications in the [traceability matrix](traceability-matrix.md#planned-verification-specifications).
