All criteria below require actual source-bound evidence and genuine independent review. This list supplies no permission to begin the work.

1. **Supported fixes or independently approved applicability decisions.** Establish a complete route for the exact candidate and every finding, followed by complete assessment with **zero unresolved Critical and zero unresolved High** under unchanged B1 thresholds. 2. **Authenticated upstream package/provenance evidence.** Verify signed repository metadata using independently trusted keys/verifier or authentic independent verification receipts; bind exact package/image/wheel hashes, dependency closure, source/build provenance, licences and notices. Enrol genuine reviewer keys and obtain current signed source-bound admission evidence. Rebind future manifests to the then-current source/docs; never alter frozen pins or fabricate trust from hashes, TLS or supplied reviewer names alone.
3. **Complete authenticated vulnerability database within the existing cap.** Retain the actual supported database bytes and prove independent authenticity, completeness, scanner/schema compatibility, **at most 1 GiB** and B1's **at most seven-day age** at admission. Expanded bytes, not compressed archive size, must meet the cap. No splitting, advisory deletion, summary/URI substitution or weakened threshold. Any lossless compaction proposal needs separate proof of logical equivalence, preserved metadata and full coverage; do not assume it fits.
4. **CPython, OpenSSL, Cargo and native-component coverage.** Cover exact CPython 3.12 patch/build features, cryptography's separately bundled OpenSSL, all declared Cargo dependencies with lock/features/target/runtime linkage, and recursively linked/static native components. Use authenticated advisories and supported mappings or substantiated independent analysis; zero-target/unsupported scans remain NOT_TESTED. Close licence, optional-module and required runtime compatibility gaps without promoting missing evidence to PASS.
5. **Separate approvals for acquisition, builds and runtime validation.** First obtain a bounded, exact acquisition approval after a complete proposal is reviewable. Successful acquisition or signed admission cannot enable the current builder. Any future builder implementation/execution needs its own explicit approval and review proving physical daemon/frontend/RUN/cache/exporter isolation. Separately approve two clean reproducibility builds and final-image SBOM/CVE checks, then bounded Linux non-root/permissions/health/API/UI/MCP tests and M20 audit/recovery/M21 revocation regressions. No automatic progression between these phases.
